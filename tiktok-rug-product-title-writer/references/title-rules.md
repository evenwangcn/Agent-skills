# Official U.S. title guidance baseline

Checked: 2026-10-14. Recheck before generation; these sources can change.

- [Product Listing Policy](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3196690250417921&role=1): policy-level title requirements, authorized brand, relevant identity, title capitalization, units and pack quantity. Size/color belong in variation titles, not the main title. Avoid seller links/names, unauthorized platform references, marketing/popularity claims, misleading attributes, and letter substitutions. Ordinary meaningful punctuation is allowed. The policy also prohibits duplicate listings for the same product.
- [Product Listing: What You Need to Know](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7073362639816491&role=1): title guidance gives 25–200 characters, accurate product details and brand format, and no keyword repetition, variant lists, decorative symbols, promotions, or inventory messaging. Its general examples allow size when applicable; for main-versus-variation placement, follow the explicit policy above.
- [Product Detail Pages & Listing Quality Guidelines](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?default_language=en&knowledge_id=481891871868714): quality guidance gives 25–255 characters. This differs from the title guide; 25–200 is a drafting range compatible with both, not a claim that every backend rejects 201 characters.

Use trends only as relevant descriptive language. Never label a title Trending Item, Best Seller, or TikTok Exclusive simply because research finds topic interest.
